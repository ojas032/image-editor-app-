Center Your Subject

Instagram crops your square image into a circle. Keep your face or logo perfectly centered to avoid cutting off important details.

Good Lighting

For personal brands, use a photo with soft, natural light on your face. Dark or backlit photos look unprofessional.

✨

Keep it Simple

Profile pictures are small! Avoid cluttered backgrounds or tiny text that nobody can read on a phone screen.

🏢

Use the same profile picture across all social media platforms to make your brand instantly recognizable.
