UPSC Photo & Signature Rules

  • Photograph: Must be a recent passport size color picture. The name of the candidate and the date on which the photograph was taken should be mentioned on the photograph.
  • Signature: Scanned signature on white paper with Black Ink pen.
  • Size: Images should be legible and clear. Blur images will lead to rejection.
