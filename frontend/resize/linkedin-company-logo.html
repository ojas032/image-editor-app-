LinkedIn Company Logo Best Practices

Learn how to create thumbnails that get more clicks and views on YouTube

👀

Eye-Catching Visuals

Use bright colors, high contrast, and emotional expressions to grab attention in the first 3 seconds.

📝

Clear Text

Keep text short and readable. Use large, bold fonts that are easy to read even when thumbnail is small.

🎭

Facial Expressions

Show emotion! Happy, surprised, or curious faces perform much better than neutral expressions.

🔍

Safe Zone

Keep important elements within the center 70% of the image. YouTube crops thumbnails on different devices.
