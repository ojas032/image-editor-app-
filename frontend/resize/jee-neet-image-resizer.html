NTA Photo Instructions

Important: The photograph should cover 80% face (without mask) visible including ears against white background.

Postcard size photograph (4" × 6") is required for JEE Main and NEET. It must be taken on or after the specified date (usually within 3 months).

Frequently Asked Questions

Please note that for JEE Main and NEET UG, candidates are required to upload two different types of photographs:
  1. Passport Size Photo: Used for the attendance sheet and potential admit card.
  2. Postcard Size Photo (4"x6"): Must be uploaded separately. This is a larger version of your photo, often required to be pasted in the exam center proforma.
Both photos usually need to be identical (same shot), just different sizes. Our tool helps you create both from the same original image.

  • Passport Photo: 10 Kb to 200 Kb
  • Postcard Photo: 10 Kb to 200 Kb
  • Signature: 4 Kb to 30 Kb
Our tool automatically attempts to optimize your image to fit these constraints.

Historically, NTA often requires the Candidate's Name and Date of Taking Photograph to be printed on the bottom of the photograph. Please check the current year's information bulletin to confirm. If required, ensure your uploaded image has this text, or edit it before resizing.
