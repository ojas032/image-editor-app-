How to Crop Images Online

✂️

Visual Cropping with Mouse

Our interactive crop tool lets you visually select the exact area you want to keep. Simply click and drag on the image to create a crop rectangle. You can:

🎯

Create

Click and drag anywhere on the image to draw the crop area

↕️

Move

Click inside the rectangle and drag to reposition it

📐

Resize

Drag the corners to resize proportionally or drag edges to resize in one direction

⚡

Fine-tune

Use the manual controls with sliders and number inputs for pixel-perfect precision

📐

Precise Manual Controls

Need exact dimensions? Use our dual-input system for maximum precision:

🎚️

Sliders

Quick adjustments for width, height, X position, and Y position

🔢

Number Inputs

Type exact pixel values for perfect accuracy

🔒

Aspect Ratio Lock

Maintain proportions while resizing

⚡

Quick Presets

One-click options for common ratios (Square 1:1, Portrait 4:5, Landscape 16:9, Story 9:16)
