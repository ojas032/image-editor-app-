Frequently Asked Questions

Why convert JPEG to WebP?

WebP provides 25-35% better compression than JPEG while maintaining the same visual quality. This results in smaller file sizes and faster loading websites.

Is WebP widely supported?

WebP is supported by all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. For older browsers, you can provide JPEG fallbacks.

Will image quality be maintained?

Yes, WebP maintains excellent image quality while providing superior compression. You can expect the same visual quality with significantly smaller file sizes.
