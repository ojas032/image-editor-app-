Frequently Asked Questions

What is a QR code?

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a two-dimensional barcode that stores information. When scanned with a smartphone camera, it instantly opens the encoded content like websites, contacts, or WiFi settings.

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes, our QR code generator is 100% free with no limits. Create unlimited QR codes for URLs, WiFi, vCards, and more without signup or hidden charges.

Do QR codes expire?

Static QR codes never expire. They work as long as the content they link to is available. A URL QR code will work as long as the website exists.

Can I add my logo to a QR code?

Yes! Upload a logo and it will be placed in the center of your QR code. Use high error correction to ensure the code still scans properly.

What formats can I download?

Download QR codes in PNG format (for web and print) or SVG format (vector, infinitely scalable for large format prints).

How do I create a WiFi QR code?

Select 'WiFi' type, enter your network name (SSID), password, and encryption. Guests can scan to connect instantly without typing the password.
