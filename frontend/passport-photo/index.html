Frequently Asked Questions

What size is a passport photo?

Passport photo sizes vary by country. US passport photos are 2x2 inches (51x51mm), UK photos are 35x45mm, and Indian passport photos are 2x2 inches. Our tool automatically applies the correct dimensions.

Is this passport photo maker free?

Yes, our passport photo maker is completely free to use. There are no hidden charges, watermarks, or signup requirements. Create unlimited passport photos for any country.

Can I print passport photos at home?

Yes! Our tool provides print-ready 4x6 inch templates with multiple passport photos that you can print at home or at any photo printing service.

What background is required for passport photos?

Most countries require a plain white or off-white background. Our tool can automatically remove your original background and replace it with white or any solid color.

Will my passport photo be accepted officially?

Our tool follows official government specifications for each country. Final acceptance depends on your original photo quality and adherence to requirements like expression and glasses.

Which countries are supported?

We support 50+ countries including USA, UK, India, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Schengen visa, China, Japan, and many more with pre-configured photo requirements.
