Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove background from an image?

Upload your image, click "Remove Background", and our AI will automatically detect and remove the background. You can then choose a transparent background or select a solid color.

Is background removal free?

Yes! ImageNerd's background remover is completely free with no limits, watermarks, or registration required.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All background removal happens locally in your browser using AI. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

What image formats are supported?

We support JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. The output is always a high-quality PNG file with transparency support.

Can I use this for product photos?

Absolutely! Our AI background remover is perfect for e-commerce product photos, creating clean images with transparent or white backgrounds.

How accurate is the AI background removal?

Our AI model is highly accurate and preserves fine details like hair, fur, and complex edges. It works best with clear subject-background separation.
